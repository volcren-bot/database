# Community scripts addendum

This addendum applies on top of the Scripter terms of service to anyone who creates a script, uploads a revision or generates a share code without being a Volcren scripter. Publish it as the `community_tos` document from the admin page. It is a draft for legal review before it goes live.

## 1. Community scripts

A script written by someone who is not a scripter is a **community script**. Community scripts are not reviewed, tested or endorsed by Volcren. They are free, and are visible only to their author, to testers and moderators, and to people the author gave a share code.

## 2. Your responsibility as an author

You are responsible for every script and revision you upload. You confirm that you wrote it or have the right to share it, that it contains no malware, no code that steals, collects or sends anyone's data, credentials or files, and nothing that harms the user's computer, the game client or other people. A script may only do what its description says.

## 3. Limits

Each account may have a limited number of community scripts and a limited amount of stored files, counting every revision. The limits may change. Authors cannot delete scripts or old revisions; ask a moderator.

## 4. Share codes

A share code gives one person access to one script, once. You must not sell codes, generate them in bulk to get around the visibility rules, or use them to distribute a script to people who have not agreed to run it. You can remove someone's access at any time.

## 5. Moderation

Volcren may hide, restrict or delete any script, revoke share codes and ban accounts, with or without notice, if a script or its author breaks these terms or the other terms of service. A banned author's scripts are hidden from everyone below moderator; unbanning restores them.

## 6. Users of community scripts

People who run a community script do so at their own risk. Volcren is not liable for what a community script does. The loader warns before the first run of each revision.

## 7. Changes

When this addendum or the Scripter terms change, authors must accept the new version before they can create scripts, upload revisions or generate share codes. Existing scripts keep running meanwhile.
